- Built a production AI chatbot as a solo project, from design to deployment, that answers questions about users, dealerships and configuration, so people can find what they need themselves.
- Designed it as an agent with LangGraph and PydanticAI: MCP servers expose the tools it can call, every tool call is secured with OAuth, and RAG with vector search grounds its answers in the right context.
- Adopted org-wide by Service Managers, Directors and teams across all stores.
