A ground-up rebuild of myKaarma's dealership software as a single app shell. Products like Communication, Payments and Inspections plug in as independent apps that the shell loads on demand: our own as micro-frontends, third-party ones in a sandbox. I'm leading it, from the architecture and the Platform SDK through to delivery across teams.
